About me

I'm a CS + AI student at Drexel University (Class of 2029), on a full-ride Global Scholars Scholarship. I came to Philadelphia from Pakistan chasing a specific kind of ambition — the kind that doesn't fit neatly in one box.

By day I'm a research developer and data AI co-op. My work lives at the intersection of knowledge graphs, large language models, and the genuinely hard question of how people learn.

By night — and on weekends — I paint, photograph, and find ways to make the world look the way it feels inside my head.
